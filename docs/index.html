What it does

Listens 0.2 ms

A log-mel frontend and a 38 KB quantised detector, one window every 480 ms, all night in a foreground service. Below −55 dBFS no inference runs at all, which is most of why it survives a night on a battery.

Acts first 12s · 20s · 35s

Logs a fuss at twelve seconds, plays a sound at twenty — your own recorded voice, if you made one — then re-reads the loudness envelope to find out whether it helped. Only then does it wake anybody, and the alert says what it tried.

Learns which sound

Every attempt is scored against what the crying actually did next, so the sound that settles this baby gets played more often. That verification step is the product.

Answers 46 sources

Field-weighted BM25 over curated entries, for the baby and for the mother. Answers are retrieved text shown with their source; a 0.5B model may shorten one, and is never allowed to supply a fact.

Reads a strip PP-OCRv4

Rotated-box detection, an angle classifier and CTC recognition, on device — so a torn blister sheet photographed upside down still reads. Then five staged checks against her own records.

Watches

Face presence and motion energy from the camera. Deliberately not pose estimation: adult-trained pose models measurably fail on infants.

Without a baby: on the Monitor tab tap Play a demo cry to run the whole ladder in twenty seconds, and on Watch tap Play demo footage to put a real clip of a crawling baby through the real detector. Both run the actual pipeline — only the source of the samples differs.

Nothing leaves the phone

  • —No network requests. Cleartext is forbidden outright in the manifest. The app holds no API keys because it calls no APIs.
  • —No audio is recorded. Monitored sound is analysed in memory and discarded. The soothing clips you record yourself are the one exception, and they stay in app-private storage.
  • —Backup is disabled, so health documents cannot leave by a cloud transport.
  • —No account, no analytics. The only outbound request possible is an optional model download, behind an explicit tap.
What it is not Nila is an awareness aid, not a medical device. It does not monitor breathing, heart rate or oxygen, and must not be relied on to detect a medical emergency. It never states a medicine dose and never diagnoses.
